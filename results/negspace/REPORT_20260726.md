**IoU is tight: mean 0.9227, sd 0.0075, range 0.0276.** Every run finished above
the 0.91 target, which is the hard floor doing its job.

**Piece count is not: mean 10.0, sd 6.4** — and that is one run. Excluding seed 9
the counts are 7,7,7,7,7,9,9,9,10, i.e. mean 8.0 and sd 1.2.

### Why seed 9 kept 28 pieces, and it is not noise

Shedding starts only after IoU holds at `count_target_iou + count_start_margin`
= 0.91 + 0.02 = **0.93** for `count_start_patience` = 2 consecutive evaluations.

Best IoU for the nine shedding runs: 0.9362, 0.9366, 0.9369, 0.9387, 0.9391,
0.9416, 0.9416, 0.9478, 0.9532 — all clear 0.93.
Best IoU for seed 9: **0.9272** — short by 0.0028.

Perfect separation. The bimodality is a deterministic threshold on peak IoU, not
stochastic shedding behaviour, and a **0.3% shortfall in peak IoU produced a 4x
difference in final piece count**. Seed 9's *final* IoU (0.9249) is actually
above the batch mean, so piece count alone identifies it; IoU alone does not.

Practical consequence: for any batch summary, report the shed/stall split
alongside the mean. A mean piece count over a mixed population describes no
actual run.

## 3. Silhouette-weight extremes: full SRD, no restart

`neg_silweight_extremes_20260725`

| pair | config | IoU | pieces | spill | coverage | precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cat_face_bass | awflip | 0.8418 | 19 | 0.1889 | 0.9992 | 0.8423 |
| cat_face_bass | **silonly** | 0.6072 | 33 | **0.6578** | **1.0000** | 0.6072 |
| sun_moon | awflip | 0.8367 | 26 | 0.2150 | 0.9984 | 0.8376 |
| sun_moon | **silonly** | 0.5262 | 45 | **0.9065** | 0.9997 | 0.5263 |
| water_fire | awflip | 0.8271 | 22 | 0.2094 | 0.9998 | 0.8273 |
| water_fire | **silonly** | 0.5238 | 35 | **0.9108** | 1.0000 | 0.5238 |

`silonly` = area-normalized, silhouette 4.5 / negative space 0.
`awflip` = area-normalized 4.194 / 0.306, i.e. aw0p073 with its weights swapped
(sil:neg 13.7).

**silonly does exactly what the loss tells it to and it is catastrophic.**
Coverage reaches **1.0000** — every target pixel filled, perfectly — while spill
runs 0.66 to 0.91, meaning on sun_moon and water_fire the geometry covers over
90% of the background as well. Precision equals IoU to four decimals in every
run, which is the arithmetic signature of coverage = 1: with no misses,
IoU = TP/(TP+FP) = precision.

It also grows pieces rather than shedding them (33–45 against a 20 start), since
nothing penalises adding material anywhere.

`awflip` at ratio 13.7 is far from that cliff but still 3–10x the spill of the
normal configs (0.19–0.22 against 0.02–0.07), for 8–12 IoU points less.

Together with the earlier results this completes a monotone series across the
whole weight axis: **effective ratio 0.032 → 0.073 → 0.19 → 0.25 → 13.7 → ∞
gives spill 0.022 → 0.039 → 0.067 → 0.077 → 0.20 → 0.83.**

Worth recording why this experiment needed area normalization. Non-normalized
`silhouette_loss` averages `(alpha - mask)^2` over the whole frame, and in the
background `mask = 0`, so the term is `alpha^2` there — it carries its own spill
penalty. At these pairs' mean foreground fraction (0.167), silhouette 4.5 /
negative space 0 run non-normalized would be equivalent to silhouette 0.75 /
negative space 3.75, an effective ratio of 0.20 — close to aw0p1875, and still
overwhelmingly negative-space weighted. The run would have been labelled "no
negative space" while being ~83% negative-space pressure.
